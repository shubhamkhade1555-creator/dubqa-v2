Pipeline Architecture
10-node dubbing pipeline — each node is a QA test target.
1
INGEST
FFmpeg probes media metadata: codec, duration, resolution, frame rate, audio sample rate.
FFmpegffprobe
2
SEPARATE (Bandit)
ML source separation splits the original audio into: dialogue.wav, music.wav, fx.wav stems.
Bandit ML
3
SENSE (Gemini)
Diarizes speakers, transcribes with word-level timestamps, detects emotion per segment.
Gemini STTTC1 source
4
TRANSLATE
Custom translation engine applies language-pair rules to produce target-language scripts.
Custom EngineTC4 input
5
VOICE (ElevenLabs)
Instant Voice Cloning from the Bandit dialogue stem. TTS synthesis per segment.
ElevenLabs IVCTC2 output
6
PRE-STITCH
Duration fitting and tempo adaptation to align synthesised audio with original timing.
Internal
7
STITCH
Synthesised speech segments placed on the full timeline with sample-accurate alignment.
InternalTC1 alignment
8
MIX
FFmpeg merges dubbed dialogue with music + FX background bed at calibrated levels.
FFmpeg
9
LIPSYNC (Sync.so)
Sync.so sync-3 model re-renders mouth movements on the video to match dubbed audio.
Sync.soTC3 target
10
MUX
FFmpeg combines the lip-synced video with the final mixed audio into localized.mp4.
FFmpegFinal output

Test Plan Guide
Every test case maps to a viewer complaint category.
TC1 STT — "They said something completely different" → P0 Critical
TC2 Emotion — "The voice sounds wrong / flat / robotic" → P1 High
TC3 Lipsync — "The mouth doesn't match the words" → P1 High
TC4 Translation — "The meaning changed / something is missing" → P0 Critical

Execution Order
INGEST → EXTRACT → TC1 → TC2 → TC3 → TC4 → EXPORT
TC2 reuses TC1 segment boundaries. TC3 reuses TC1 word timestamps. TC4 reuses TC1 transcripts.
